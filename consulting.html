Service 01

Setting up & scaling your
Support & Calling Team

From your first support hire to a full-fledged 50-person team, we design the structure, processes, and culture that make support a competitive advantage.

The challenge

Most startups build support reactively

A single agent handling everything in a shared inbox. No triage, no escalation path, no training, no SLAs. It works — until it doesn't. When volume spikes, quality collapses, agents burn out, and customers churn.

We help you get ahead of that curve with a purpose-built support structure that scales with your growth.

Who this is for

Founders or Ops leads at seed-to-Series B startups who are ready to move beyond the "everyone does support" phase and build a proper team — whether that's 2 people or 20.

What we deliver

A support org built to last

  • Support org chart and team structure design
  • Role definitions: Tier-1, Tier-2, Team Lead, QA, Trainer
  • Hiring process: JDs, interview scorecards, sourcing strategy
  • 30/60/90 day onboarding and training plans
  • KPI framework: CSAT, FRT, AHT, FCR benchmarks
  • Shift planning and capacity modelling
  • Inbound calling team setup: IVR, call routing, scripts
  • Quality monitoring and coaching frameworks
Our process

How we work with you

1

Discovery & audit

We spend time understanding your product, customer base, ticket volumes, and current support pain points through structured interviews and data review.

2

Design & planning

We design your team structure, define roles, map out processes, and create a phased hiring and training plan aligned with your growth roadmap.

3

Documentation handover

You receive a complete playbook — SOPs, hiring kits, onboarding tracks, and KPI dashboards — ready to execute from day one.

4

Ongoing advisory (optional)

We offer retainer-based advisory to review metrics, coach team leads, and adapt your strategy as you scale further.

Pricing is custom & on request

Every engagement is scoped individually based on team size, complexity, and timelines. We'll put together a transparent proposal after a free 30-minute discovery call — no commitment required.

Service 02

Templates for
Support Best Practices

Stop reinventing the wheel every time you onboard someone or handle a tough ticket. We build your team's entire playbook library — ready to use and easy to maintain.

Why it matters

Consistency is the foundation of great support

Without documented processes, every agent handles tickets differently. Quality depends on who's working that day. New hires take months to ramp. Customer experience is unpredictable.

Our template packages give your team a shared language, a clear process, and the confidence to handle anything that comes their way.

Delivered as editable documents

All templates are delivered in formats your team can actually use — Google Docs, Notion, or your preferred tool. We customize the tone and content to match your brand and product.

Template library

What's included

Response templates

Macros & canned responses

50+ pre-written responses for your most common ticket types, with tone guidance and customization notes.

SOPs

Escalation playbook

Clear decision trees for when and how to escalate — from Tier 1 to Tier 2 to Product/Engineering.

Onboarding

New agent training guide

A structured 30-day onboarding track with quizzes, shadowing schedules, and readiness criteria.

Service 03

CRM & Ticketing
Setup & Configuration

The right tool, configured the right way. We help you pick, set up, and optimize your CRM and helpdesk — so your team can focus on customers, not admin.

The problem

Bad setup creates bad data — and bad support

Many teams pick a tool quickly, set it up in an afternoon, and never revisit it. The result: tickets fall through cracks, automation breaks, reports are meaningless, and agents work around the tool instead of with it.

We take your CRM setup seriously — because it's the backbone of every customer interaction your team has.

What we set up

End-to-end implementation

  • Tool evaluation and recommendation for your stack
  • Channel setup: email, chat, phone, WhatsApp, social
  • Ticket categorisation, tagging, and priority rules
  • Automation: auto-assign, SLA rules, escalation triggers
  • CSAT survey setup and response rate optimisation
  • Reporting dashboards: volume, SLAs, agent performance
  • CRM pipeline setup: lead stages, deal flows, custom fields
  • Integration: connect your CRM with helpdesk, billing, product
  • Team training and admin handover documentation
Engagement model

How our CRM implementation works

1

Needs assessment

We map your current tools, workflows, team structure, and integrations to understand your exact requirements before recommending anything.

2

Tool selection & scoping

If you haven't chosen a tool yet, we evaluate options against your needs and budget. If you already have one, we audit your current setup and create an optimisation roadmap.

3

Build & configure

We handle the full setup — channels, automations, pipelines, dashboards, integrations. You get a working system, not just a plan.

4

Train & handover

We train your team, document every configuration, and hand over admin access with full context. You'll own it completely after we're done.

5

Post-launch support

We offer a hyper-care period after launch to fix teething issues, and optional ongoing retainer support for changes and scale-ups.
