QA Sentinel

Every ticket reviewed. Not just the ones you have time for.

Most teams spot-check 2–3% of tickets. QA Sentinel uses Claude to score 100% of them against your actual SOPs — consistently, instantly, and without a QA team working overtime.

Try QA Sentinel Free
  • AI scoring against your SOPs — SOP Adherence, Tone & Empathy, Accuracy, Resolution Quality, and Response Completeness, scored 0–100 with a written rationale.
  • Bulk review — score dozens of tickets in one pass instead of one at a time.
  • Calibration workflows — multiple reviewers blind-score the same ticket, then compare results to keep the whole team applying the rubric consistently.
  • Agent scorecards — trend performance per agent over time, not just a flat list of scores.
  • DSAT analysis — automatic root-cause breakdowns on dissatisfied customers.
  • Zendesk connection — pulls ticket conversations directly, no copy-pasting transcripts.
  • Configurable pipeline — define your own stages and mark any stage terminal; not locked into someone else's sales process.
  • Custom fields — add exactly the fields your business needs, no hardcoded schema to work around.
  • Journeys & automation — trigger-based workflows for stage changes, follow-ups, and waits.
  • Task management with SLA tracking, so nothing slips through.
  • CSV import for your existing leads and contacts — no re-typing your pipeline.
  • Audit log & data export built in from day one.
